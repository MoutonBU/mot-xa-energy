# MCP Architecture and Interiors XA Energy - Release notes

Current version: **1.35.8**. You can read these notes in the program: File > Release notes... The version number is shown in the installer, in the installer file name
(`MCP_XA_Energy_Setup_x.y.z.exe`) and in Windows' list of installed programs.

## What is new since the first version (1.0.0)

### New features
- **Coverage & FAR tab** (beside Energy XA): stand area, blocks, carport roofs; coverage % and floor area ratio for
  one block, all blocks, and all blocks with carport roofs; PASS/FAIL against the limits you enter; areas filled from
  the Rooms list (storeys and floor areas per level from the IFC); choice of net rooms + walls, IFC gross, or the gross
  floor area from Building details; its own PDF report.
- **One PDF per subject**: every report (Light & Ventilation, Energy XA, Rational Design, Coverage & FAR) can be exported
  as a separate PDF for each subject (Reports menu > "Export each subject as its own PDF...").
- **Cover page** on every report: logo, project, site, location, client, architect and who compiled it.
- **Sign-in and licences**: accounts with email and password; a licence is created automatically for every new
  account (no key to type); licences can be blocked, extended or expired; the key is also emailed; users who are signed
  in stay signed in.
- **Updates**: the red "Update available" button and Menu > Check for updates download and install the new version in
  one click, with no token. Your work is saved and restored after the update.
- **Dark / light mode** switch that changes the interface at once, without restarting. The installer matches the dark theme.
- **Search, filters and Next problem (1.31.0):** the notes list now has a search box (type DPC or insulation) and filter buttons: All, Checked, To verify, Unfinished, each with a count. The number of each note is coloured by status (green checked, red to verify, amber unfinished). New “Next problem” button jumps to the next note that still needs you and brings it into view on the drawing. The status line shows the percentage checked.
- **Workflow rail, project dashboard and submission checklist (1.32.0):** the Detail Notes tab has a left rail (Open, Auto note, Review, Export) with progress and a Next problem button; it hides on narrow windows. After Batch folder a Project dashboard opens: counts, one card per detail with a progress bar, the same-material-different-wording warning, and a submission checklist (waterproofing/DPC, roof and wall R-values against your climate zone, foundation depth, products to verify, specifications chosen). “Build submission pack” gathers the noted drawings, each detail's schedule, the project schedule and the checklist into one folder. The checklist only reads what the notes say; you still approve each note.
- **Light & Ventilation (1.33.1):** the experimental “5  Plan” tab is removed. Light & Ventilation is back to Windows, Doors, Rooms and Results.
- **New layout: left rail and ribbon (1.34.0):** the tabs across the top are replaced by a rail on the left (your project and site, then Overview, Light & ventilation with Windows / Doors / Rooms / Results under it, Energy (XA), Coverage & FAR and Detail notes, with the pass count beside Light & ventilation) and a ribbon of grouped commands above the page (Project, Model, Check, Reports, with icons). Run check is highlighted; Preview PDF, Export PDF and All subjects sit in Reports. The branded header is slimmer to give the pages more room. Everything that was on the tabs is still there and works the same; the rail hides on very narrow windows. “Build submission pack” at the bottom of the rail opens the Detail Notes project dashboard.
- **Plan shows every door and the gates (1.35.8):** doors that the model keeps only as a 2D symbol (the D1 and D3 doors into kitchens, bedrooms and bathrooms) had no 3D shape and were missing; they are now placed from the door's own position and width in the IFC and show with their tag. Gates (doors named G) are no longer hidden: they appear in light grey with their tag, and the key lists Door and Gate. The PDF floor plans carry the same.
- **Plan viewport: steadier and easier to move around (1.35.7):** selecting a room no longer makes window and door tags vanish (the plan is now fully redrawn on every change). A selected room gets a thick bright outline with a light wash; hovering shows a thin outline and a tip with the room's name, area and result. A click only selects when you did not drag, so moving the plan no longer changes the selection. You can pan left, right, up and down with the arrow buttons next to Fit, the arrow keys, Shift + wheel (sideways), by dragging, or with the scroll bars; zoom with the wheel, the - and + buttons or the keys; double-click or F fits the level; Esc clears the selection. There is room to pan beyond the plan and a hint line under it.
- **Plan colours on every level (1.35.6):** the ground floor stayed uncoloured when the same combined room name (for example "Kitchen / Lounge") had been used in two units, because the program remembers only one list of parts per name. A combined room now takes its level and unit from its own result row, and a combined room that still cannot be matched is matched through the room names listed in its own name. Results are also matched when they name a level differently from the model ("Level 1" against "Ground Floor"), by unit, name and area. Tested with the Block A model and with six levels. Levels with no rooms are marked "(no rooms)" in the level list.
- **Plan follows combined rooms; cleaner drawing (1.35.5):** rooms combined on the Rooms tab (open plan, e.g. Kitchen + Lounge) now show on the plan as every part of the combined room in the combined room's colour, and the room list says which combined room a part belongs to. This applies to the PDF floor plans as well. Zones without a result are drawn faint and dashed underneath the rooms, so they can no longer hide a coloured room, and room labels are placed so that none is written on top of another (tiny zones such as passages are left to the list and tooltip when the label would not fit). Labels are centred on the room's own centre.
- **Plan colours fixed, every zone picked up (1.35.4):** the pass / check / fail colours were not being applied because the program drops the unit prefix from room names ("U3-Kitchen" is "Kitchen" in unit U3) while the plan used the full name. Rooms are now matched to the results on level, unit and bare name, and by closest area when a name repeats (the several "Pass." passages). Passages, stoeps and patios, which the model keeps as thin sheets near the ceiling, were missing from the plan; every IfcSpace is now drawn from its outline seen from above, and one that was lost (U3-Main Bedroom) is back. The PDF floor plans use the same matching.
- **Plan: clearer windows and doors, no gates, PDF switch (1.35.3):** windows and doors are drawn bolder and each carries its tag from the model (W1, D2...) on a coloured chip; the Tags box on the Plan tab hides them. A door whose name starts with G (G1, G2...) is a gate and is left off the plan. A new tick box, "Include floor plans in the PDF report", on the Results tab and on the Plan tab (they stay in step and are remembered) switches the floor plan pages off or on in the Light & ventilation PDFs.
- **Plan redrawn from the model's own geometry (1.35.2):** windows and doors are now drawn as clean bars the thickness of their wall, taken from each element's own position and width in the IFC (no more open sashes, handles or arrow shapes). Windows show a glass line, doors a leaf line, and every room is labelled with its name and area. Rooms, walls and openings still come straight from the IFC cut, so the plan matches the model.
- **Plan: every window, key panel and PDF (1.35.1):** high-level windows (bathrooms) are now cut through their own height, so none go missing, and windows and doors are drawn as thick as the wall they sit in. The Plan tab has a panel on the right with a key (passes / check / fails / no result, window, door, wall, stair), the pass-check-fail count for the level, and a list of the level's rooms with their light and ventilation result; click a room in the list or on the plan to outline it and see its numbers. The Light & ventilation PDF now includes a "Floor plan" page for each level, coloured by result, with a key.
- **Icons everywhere, Doors, Plan view and Detail notes ribbon (1.35.0):** every item on the left rail now has its icon (Overview, Light & ventilation, Energy, Coverage & FAR, Detail notes), and so do Windows, Doors, Rooms, Results and the new Plan under Light & ventilation. Doors is now in the Light & ventilation ribbon. Energy has its own ribbon (Run XA check, Report details, a Sections menu that jumps to Walls, Roof, Floors and so on, Preview, Export, All subjects), Coverage & FAR has Fill from rooms, Preview, Export, Each calculation and Site sheets, and Detail notes now has a ribbon (Open DWG / DXF, Settings, Outputs, Auto note, Next problem, Undo, Redo, Dashboard, Batch folder, Notes schedule). The Detail notes buttons and workflow steps have vector icons.

- **Plan view for Light & Ventilation (1.33.0):** a new tab “5  Plan” beside Results. ### Improvements
- Light & Ventilation PASS/FAIL results update by themselves after any change to windows, doors or rooms.
- Changing a window's width or height now changes its glazed area, opening size and opening percentage with it.
- Lighting check follows the occupancy class (SANS 10400-XA Table 12) instead of always using the residential 4 W/m2.
- Installer is readable (dark theme, summary box), names the version and replaces older copies and shortcuts.
- **Faster reports**: the 3D model is read once instead of for every PDF. A Rational Design PDF takes about 0.4 s instead of
  11 s after the first one, and "export each subject" for that report takes about 2 s instead of about 100 s.
- Keep me signed in is more reliable: the saved sign-in is only removed when the licence is switched off.

### Renamed
- The program is now **MCP Architecture and Interiors XA Energy** (was "MOT XA Energy"): window title, installer,
  shortcuts, program file (`MCP_XA_Energy.exe`) and your work folder (`MCP_XA_Energy`; the old folder is renamed, nothing is lost).

## What stays the same
- SANS 10400-O natural light and ventilation checks, window / door / room schedules, IFC and PDF import.
- SANS 10400-XA energy: envelope R-values, fenestration, orientation and shading, air leakage, hot water and solar,
  lighting and power, energy zones and the rational design report.
- Archicad PDF placement, simulation export (EnergyPlus IDF), weather files.

## Standards used (checked against the published text)
- SANS 10400-XA:2021 Table 1 (annual consumption), Table 2 (demand), Table 4 (fenestration), Table 12 (lighting power),
  wall / roof / floor R-values by energy zone, pipe insulation, hot water 50 % rule.
- SANS 10400-O: ventilation 5 % of floor area (0.2 m2 minimum). Confirm the natural-light percentage and category
  rule in your copy of the standard before submission.
- Every report states that it supports, and does not replace, the competent person's declaration.

## Quality check done for this release
- Static scan of the whole program: no undefined names, no crashes.
- Every menu action, button, tab, drop-down and theme switch was run against a real model: no errors.
- Speed: Light & Ventilation check 0.02 s, Energy XA calculation 0.12 s, Energy XA PDF 0.3 s, Coverage PDF 0.2 s.
  The first Rational Design PDF of a session needs about 10 s to read the 3D model; the dark/light switch takes about 2 s.
- Standards tables compared with the published SANS 10400-XA:2021 text: consumption and demand tables, fenestration
  table, R-values by zone, hot water and pipe insulation all match.

## Upgrading
Install the latest `MCP_XA_Energy_Setup_x.y.z.exe` once. It replaces the old program in place and removes the old
shortcuts. After that, use the update button inside the program.
